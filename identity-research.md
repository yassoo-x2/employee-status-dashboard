# Syrian visual identity research notes

Reviewed sources on 2026-09-02:

1. Saeed Studio, “هوية سورية البصرية | شعار العقاب السوري”:
   https://saeedstudio.com/هوية-سورية-البصرية/
   Reports primary colors of green and gold; secondary colors dark red, white, black, and gray. Describes the golden eagle/hawk mark, three stars, fourteen wing feathers, and five tail branches. Reports Qomra as the identity typeface, but it may be licensed/paid, so the dashboard should not embed it without rights.

2. Syrian News / الإخبارية السورية, “دلالات وطنية في الهوية البصرية الجديدة للجمهورية العربية السورية”:
   https://alikhbariah.com/الهوية-البصرية-الجديدة-للجمهورية-العربية/
   Describes the golden eagle emblem, three stars representing the people, fourteen feathers corresponding to Syrian governorates, and five tail feathers representing geographic regions. It emphasizes continuity, unity, and a modern state serving its people.

Application decision:
Use an identity-inspired institutional system with deep Syrian green, muted golden sand, dark red, white, charcoal, and gray. Use the motif language sparingly as decorative geometry; do not reproduce a protected emblem or claim official certification. Keep IBM Plex Sans Arabic as the accessible fallback unless licensed identity type is supplied by the user.
