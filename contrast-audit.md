# Palette contrast audit

Text-bearing combinations are checked against WCAG AA normal-text target 4.5:1.
Gold (#c9ab72) is used only as a non-text accent/series color; text on gold is not used.

- green text/icon on white: #0b5d4d on #ffffff = 7.82:1 [PASS]
- dark red text/icon on white: #8d2638 on #ffffff = 8.51:1 [PASS]
- charcoal chart/text on white: #33423f on #ffffff = 10.54:1 [PASS]
- gray chart/text on white: #66736f on #ffffff = 4.94:1 [PASS]
- green on sand background: #0b5d4d on #f5f1e8 = 6.93:1 [PASS]
